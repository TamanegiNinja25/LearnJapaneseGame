Kanji writing practice: write every kanji stroke by stroke with your finger.

## What's new

- **書き (Writing):** a new screen in the sidebar, and a 書く練習 button on every kanji in 漢字.
  - **なぞる (Trace):** write over a faint model; a dot marks where the next stroke starts.
  - **見ないで書く (From memory):** write from the readings, the meaning and a word that uses the
    kanji (shown in kana), so you link the word to the kanji, not just copy shapes.
  - Every stroke is checked for order, shape, position and direction. A wrong stroke flashes
    red; a second miss shows the right stroke. ヒント shows it at once, and 書き順を見る
    animates the stroke order.
  - After tracing a new kanji, 見ないでもう一度 has you write it from memory straight away.
- **New optional skill:** 書き gets its own bar on ホーム, marked 任意. It rises as you write
  the stage's kanji from memory without mistakes, adds a little to 漢字, and can appear in
  次にやること. It is not part of any stage exam, so your exam progress is unchanged.
- All 2,230 kanji in the game have stroke data.
- Installs over v0.4.0 on Android and keeps your progress. If you have v0.4.0, the game's
  update popup offers this version at start.
