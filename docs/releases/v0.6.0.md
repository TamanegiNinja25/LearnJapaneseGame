Backups, sound, kana and word writing, a new 表現 screen, much more N3–N1 vocabulary, and a Mac build.

## What's new

- **Back up your progress:** 設定 › データ › バックアップをコピー copies your whole progress as one
  line of text. Keep it in a note or e-mail, then use クリップボードから復元 on a new phone or after
  reinstalling. On Windows, Linux and Mac you can also save and load a backup file. Android now
  offers to keep your data if you uninstall.
- **Sound:** soft sounds for right and wrong answers, accepted strokes, finished kanji and rank-ups,
  plus an optional quiet background loop (設定 › サウンド; music is off by default and fades out
  while Japanese is spoken). All sounds are original.
- **Write kana and whole words:** 書き now has four sources: 漢字, ひらがな, カタカナ and 言葉. Words
  are written character by character from their reading and meaning. A new daily quest,
  字を5つ書く, rewards writing practice.
- **表現 (Natural Japanese):** a new screen with two drills. 言い換え rewrites a sentence into another
  style (polite, casual or honorific); 不自然な文 asks which reply a native speaker would not say.
- **More content:** about 950 new words across N3 (646 total), N2 (476) and N1 (505), 20 new N3
  grammar points, and a kanji list for the Mastery stage (157 jōyō kanji beyond N1).
- **Better English meanings:** fixed words that showed the wrong or a misleading meaning, such as
  いる ("to be", not "to shoot"), 肉 ("meat"), いちご ("strawberry"), 野菜 and お釣り.
- **Study reminder:** ホーム shows a banner when you haven't practised yet today.
- **Mac build:** a universal app for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs (unsigned: right-click it and
  choose Open the first time).
- The update popup now downloads the right file for Windows, Linux and Mac too.
- Installs over v0.5.0 on Android and keeps your progress.
