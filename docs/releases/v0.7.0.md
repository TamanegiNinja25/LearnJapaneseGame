Much more N3, N2 and N1 practice, and fairer quizzes.

## What's new

- **More to study at N3, N2 and N1:** each stage gains 20 grammar lessons (with 6-question
  quizzes), 15 sentence-building prompts, 3 conversation scenarios, 5 reading passages and 5
  anchor sentences. N2 also gains 35 more words.
  - N3: 646 words, 35 grammar lessons, 5 conversations, 8 passages
  - N2: 511 words, 35 grammar lessons, 5 conversations, 8 passages
  - N1: 506 words, 36 grammar lessons, 5 conversations, 8 passages
  - New conversations include a lost bag at the station, a noise complaint, a job interview,
    rescheduling a client meeting, a client apology and a thesis meeting with a professor.
- **Whole game:** 3,152 words, 205 grammar lessons and 36 conversation scenarios.
- **Fairer quizzes:** grammar lessons and exams now shuffle the answer options. Before, the right
  answer was usually the first option.
- Installs over v0.6.0 on Android and keeps your progress.
