A Japanese-first language-learning RPG. You level up from N5 to N1 and on to Mastery by proving
what you know across six skills (語彙・文法・読解・聴解・会話・漢字). Every kanji has furigana.

## Downloads

| File | Platform | How to run |
|---|---|---|
| `NihongoNoSekai-v0.2.0-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | Unzip and run `NihongoNoSekai.exe`. SmartScreen may warn because the build is unsigned: choose "More info › Run anyway". |
| `NihongoNoSekai-v0.2.0-android.apk` | Android 7.0+ (arm64 / x86_64) | Copy to the phone and open it; allow "Install unknown apps". Debug-signed for sideloading. |

| `NihongoNoSekai-v0.2.0-web.zip` | Browser / itch.io | Upload to itch.io as an HTML5 game, or serve the folder over HTTP. |

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists checksums for every file.

## What's in it

- 1,539 words (772 at N5, covering all 103 N5 kanji), 127 grammar lessons with quizzes,
  17 conversation scenarios, reading passages and multi-question anchor sentences
- Dictionary with 学習する and a daily new-word budget, Leitner spaced-repetition review with typed recall
- Sentence builder with three difficulty tiers and particle-specific feedback
- Listening with your system's Japanese voice, plus microphone shadowing
- Stage gate exams, a daily 10-question check, and daily quests
- An explorable low-poly 3D town (KayKit assets) with touch controls on phones
- Light/dark theme and an accessibility font (BIZ UDPGothic)

## Notes

- Listening needs a Japanese text-to-speech voice. Windows: Settings › Time & language › Speech ›
  Add voices › Japanese. Android: Google Speech Services › Japanese.
- N4–N1 and Mastery are starter content packs; N5 is complete.
