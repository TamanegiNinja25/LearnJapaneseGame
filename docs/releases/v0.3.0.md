Phone-friendly layout, English help for beginners, and more N5/N4 content.

## What's new

- **Fits phone screens:** columns use the full width, titles sit on one line on short screens, and
  設定 › 文字の大きさ sets the text size (automatic is larger on touch screens).
- **Touch scrolling everywhere:** drag any list or page, even when the drag starts on a button; the
  dictionary list opens a word only on a tap.
- **English until N4:** while you are at N5, English appears under every navigation item, button,
  heading, word tile, dictionary row and conversation answer. Turn it off in 設定.
- **More content:** N4 grows to 630 words, 38 grammar points, 46 sentence prompts, 7 scenarios and
  12 reading passages; N5 gains 5 scenarios, 8 passages and 24 prompts. All 27 scenarios now have
  English translations for every answer.
- **No keyboard pop-ups on phones:** typed answers open the keyboard only when you tap the field,
  and the page scrolls it into view.
- **Updates keep your progress:** APKs are now signed with a fixed project key. If you installed
  v0.2.0 from GitHub, uninstall it once before installing this version (later updates install over
  it normally).
