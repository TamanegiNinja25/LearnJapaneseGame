Speaking practice: answer out loud.

## What's new

- **Answer by speaking (Android):** every conversation turn has a ● 話して答える button. Say your
  answer instead of tapping it. Saying the right reply (or another accepted wording) counts as
  choosing it; saying one of the scripted wrong or unnatural replies gets that reply's feedback.
- **Hands-free conversations:** turn on 設定 › 会話をハンズフリーで. The game listens by itself
  after the other person has spoken and moves on after a correct answer, so a whole conversation
  can run without touching the screen.
- **Speak in 表現:** rewrite drills have ● 話して言い換える, so you can say the new style out loud.
- **Shadowing check:** ● 話して確認 shows how much of the sentence your phone understood
  (for example 「○ 一致：92%」), next to your own rating.
- **New daily quest:** 声で5回答える (answer five times by speaking).
- 設定 shows whether speech recognition is available on your device.
- The Android app is now 74 MB (smaller than before).
- Installs over v0.7.0 on Android and keeps your progress.

## How it works and privacy

Speaking practice uses your phone's own speech recognition (usually Google's). It tries
on-device recognition first; if your phone has no offline Japanese speech pack, the phone's service
may process the audio online. The game itself never sends, stores or records your voice. To keep
it fully on your phone, install the offline pack: Settings › Google › Voice / Speech › Offline
speech recognition › Japanese. The first time you press a speak button, Android asks for
microphone permission.

Speaking is Android-only for now: desktop and web builds have no speech recogniser, so their speak
buttons are hidden. Recognition checks that your words were understood; it does not grade pitch
accent or vowel length.
